# Their account (paraphrased)

The poster says their ME/CFS worsened from mild to moderate in 2026; after pushing too hard in late June they had a multi-day bedbound crash. They report strictly pacing since late June/August with a wearable tracker, keeping to about 1,800–3,000 steps per day and a heart-rate ceiling of 105–110 bpm. They are upright or active for about 6 hours a day and spend the rest resting or using a lying-down laptop setup. ^q1

At their reported baseline, symptoms are worst immediately on waking and ease toward the evening. Sitting at the computer after breakfast for 30–60 minutes builds lower-back pain and fatigue, forcing them to lie flat. ^q2

# What helped

The poster explicitly says strict pacing has stabilized their baseline, stopped the big crashes, and reduced constant flu-like symptoms compared with the summer. They use a wearable tracker, step range, and heart-rate ceiling to set limits, but still report fatigue, muscle soreness after minimal exertion, and a low plateau rather than full recovery. ^q3

# What made it worse

They report a brutal multi-day bedbound crash after pushing too hard in late June. Their account leaves uncertain whether earlier recurrent viral infections or PEM with a sore throat led to the worsening that began in January. ^q4

# Quotes

> **OP:** “Strict pacing has definitely helped. My constant flu-like symptoms have dialled down compared to the summer, and I’m avoiding the massive multi-week crashes.” ^q5

> **OP:** “My daily energy envelope is around 1,800–3,000 steps, and I try to keep my heart rate under 105–110 bpm.” ^q6
