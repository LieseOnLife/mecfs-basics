# Post-exertional malaise

>[!warning] Evidence log, not medical advice
>This page explains what research found and what people report. It doesn't recommend treatments. Talk through any change with a clinician who knows ME/CFS.

> [!summary] Quick answer
> Post-exertional malaise (PEM) is a worsening of symptoms and function after physical or mental exertion that used to be tolerated.[[🔬Beyond Myalgic Encephalomyelitis-Chronic Fatigue Syndrome Redefining an Illness -by- Institute of Medicine#^q24|IOM 2015]] It's often delayed by hours or days, is out of proportion to the activity, and recovery can take hours, days, weeks, or longer.[[🔬Myalgic encephalomyelitis (or encephalopathy)-chronic fatigue syndrome diagnosis and management NG206 -by- National Institute for Health and Care Excellence#^q18|NICE 2021]][[🔬Myalgic encephalomyelitis (or encephalopathy)-chronic fatigue syndrome diagnosis and management NG206 -by- National Institute for Health and Care Excellence#^q19|q19]][[🔬Myalgic encephalomyelitis (or encephalopathy)-chronic fatigue syndrome diagnosis and management NG206 -by- National Institute for Health and Care Excellence#^q20|q20]][[🔬Myalgic encephalomyelitis (or encephalopathy)-chronic fatigue syndrome diagnosis and management NG206 -by- National Institute for Health and Care Excellence#^q21|q21]] The Clinician Coalition calls it the hallmark and most distinctive symptom of ME/CFS.[[🔬Diagnosing and Treating Myalgic Encephalomyelitis-Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (ME-CFS) -by- US ME-CFS Clinician Coalition#^q7|Coalition 2020]] In the largest two-day exercise study, people with ME/CFS couldn't repeat their first-day performance 24 hours later, unlike sedentary controls,[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q2|Keller 2024]] though many of the differences were small to moderate in size.[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q7|Keller 2024]]

## What PEM is
- **Triggered by exertion of any kind:** physical, cognitive, and emotional effort, and also orthostatic, environmental, and sensory stress.[^bhc-pem] (Orthostatic means related to being upright.[^coal-orthostatic]) For some people, even basic daily activities can trigger it.[^coal-crash]
- **Out of proportion:** the worsening is disproportionate to the activity.[^nice-pem]
- **What it feels like:** people often describe "flu-like symptoms" (profound fatigue, weakness, fever, headache, dizziness, sore throat, muscle and joint pain), worse cognitive symptoms, and more sensitivity to noise, light, and other stimuli.[^bhc-symptoms]
- **"Crash":** patients often call it a crash, relapse, or collapse.[^coal-crash] The Bateman Horne Center uses "crash" for periods of prolonged, severe PEM.[^bhc-symptoms]

## When it starts (the delay)
The delay is part of what makes PEM hard to spot. These sources describe when it starts, not why it's delayed, and their estimates vary:

| Source | Onset after exertion |
|---|---|
| CDC | Symptoms typically worsen 12 to 48 hours after activity, and PEM can last days or weeks.[^cdc-pem] |
| Bateman Horne Center | Often 12 to 72 hours after the trigger, lasting hours to weeks, or even months.[^bhc-pem] |
| Clinician Coalition | May be immediate, but more often delayed by hours or days.[^coal-crash] |
| ME Association | Can be delayed by 24 hours or more.[^mea-delay] |

Some patients don't recognize PEM at the start of their illness.[^cdc-unrecognized] Because PEM may be delayed after its trigger, the IOM suggests a week or two of activity and symptom diary (see [[📘ME-CFS - Diagnosis and getting care#Helping someone prepare]]).[^iom-diary]

> [!quote] 🗣️ Lived experience: r/cfs, 2026
> One person describes the delay after a weekend away: "I went on a tiring trip Fri-Sun and thought I was fine on Mon (which I celebrated)" and then "by Tues night I started with horrible symptoms… GI issues, extreme fatigue, brain fog, body aches, and so on…" One account, not a measurement of timing.[[🗣️Delayed crash after a tiring trip -by- r-cfs member#^q4|r/cfs, 2026]][[🗣️Delayed crash after a tiring trip -by- r-cfs member#^q5|q5]]

## Recovery
Returning to baseline after a crash can take hours, days, a week, or longer.[^coal-crash] Severe crashes can leave someone unable to walk, talk, eat, drink, or sit upright, and can become life-threatening.[^bhc-severe] What carers can do during a crash is on [[📘ME-CFS - Daily life and caring at home#During a crash]].

## The evidence: two-day exercise testing
The IOM report says reports of PEM are supported by objective evidence, including failure to reproduce results on a 2-day cardiopulmonary exercise test (CPET) and impaired thinking after exertion.[^iom-pem]

**How the test works:** the first test measures baseline capacity and acts as a standard stressor; the second, 24 hours later, measures how much function changed because of the post-exertional response.[^stevens-design] Healthy and other patient groups normally reproduce their results, so failing to do so despite peak effort on both days suggests impaired recovery.[^stevens-repeat] The IOM notes the test itself may cause a severe worsening of symptoms.[^iom-cpet]

> [!example] 🔬 Study spotlight: Keller 2024, the largest 2-day CPET study[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q3|Keller 2024]]
> 84 people meeting the Canadian Consensus Criteria (CCC) and 71 sedentary controls did two maximum-effort cycling tests 24 hours apart.[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q1|Keller 2024]][[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q3|q3]][[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q5|q5]] Unlike the controls, the ME/CFS group couldn't reproduce their first-day results, with significant declines in work, exercise time, oxygen use, heart rate, and other measures on day two.[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q2|Keller 2024]] The pattern held in 55 pairs matched for aerobic capacity, age, and sex, which the authors say shows fitness level doesn't explain it.[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q1|Keller 2024]][[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q3|q3]][[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q5|q5]] The ME/CFS group's oxygen use at the ventilatory/anaerobic threshold (VAT), which the authors describe as a pivotal shift in oxygen transport and possibly energy metabolism, fell on day two.[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q6|Keller 2024]] They say this drop “effectively shrinks the ‘energy envelope’” (see [[📘ME-CFS - Daily life and caring at home#Pacing and the energy envelope]]).[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q9|Keller 2024]] Limits: the authors call sample size the main limitation, and many effect sizes were small to moderate.[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q7|Keller 2024]] The ME/CFS participants were over 90% Caucasian and mostly mildly to moderately ill, and sex differences weren't analyzed, so the results may not apply to all races and ethnicities or to people with severe ME/CFS.[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q8|Keller 2024]] Four of the authors run 2-day CPETs for a fee.[[🔬Cardiopulmonary and metabolic responses during a 2-day CPET in ME-CFS -by- Betsy Keller#^q10|Keller 2024]]

A 2020 meta-analysis pooled 5 small studies with 98 patients and 51 controls; all used the Fukuda definition, under which PEM is optional,[^vink-fukuda] and two also used the International Consensus Criteria (ICC) or the CCC.[^lim-studies] (Why the definition matters: [[📘ME-CFS - Reading the research]].) In all four measures, patients' average results fell on day 2 while controls' rose.[^lim-direction] The patient–control difference was significant for workload at the ventilatory threshold (p < 0.05).[^lim-results] The authors note the small number of participants and studies as a limitation.[^lim-limits] The authors call the 2-day CPET a potential objective assessment of PEM but say larger studies must compare it with other fatiguing conditions, including depression.[^lim-validate]

**Interpreting this:** these studies measure PEM in research settings. The Coalition says no test is yet validated for diagnosing ME/CFS in clinical practice,[^coal-notest] and the IOM guide, which describes the 2-day CPET, warns it may severely worsen symptoms.[^iom-cpet] Authors of both the Keller study[^keller-coi] and a methods paper[^stevens-coi] run 2-day CPETs for a fee.
