## Their account (paraphrased)

The writer says they had CFS and low energy for nearly 30 years, with symptoms mild until about four years before the post; they received a CFS diagnosis only recently after a two-night sleep study. The doctor did not discuss the results in person due to time, said she would call but did not, and the writer requested the results and received them after a few weeks. ^q1

They did not initially recognize PEM because they could go to the gym and sometimes felt better after a light workout. Looking back, they associate prolonged speaking or working for two days straight with a sore throat or crashing on the couch, and say even holidays were difficult. Trainer-designed serious exercise programs repeatedly led to crashes within days and weeks of feeling ill. ^q2

## What helped

Reading posts in the community helped the writer make sense of their past symptoms, and they say they wish they had known about CFS earlier; they point to pacing as important, but do not report actually following a pacing plan. ^q3

## What made it worse

The writer reports trainer-designed programs followed by crashes within days and weeks of illness, as well as symptoms after prolonged speaking and work. They also describe waiting weeks to obtain sleep-study results after the promised call did not happen. ^q4
