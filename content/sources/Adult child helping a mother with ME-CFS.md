## Their account (paraphrased)

An adult child says their mother has been ill with ME/CFS since the child was 9; at 23, the child remembers her spending much of her time in bed or in a dark bedroom, with too little energy to leave the house. Even when drained, she remained a source of comfort when the child was upset. ^q1

After moving about a 20-minute drive away, the child helps with groceries and sometimes cooking and cleaning, and visits when studies allow. The mother gets few visits apart from the child, the child's father, and a cleaning person. The child feels guilty about moving and prioritizing studies, and says the mother's symptoms have worsened; she was too sick to celebrate Christmas with the family. ^q2

## What helped

The practical support described is visiting, groceries, and occasional cooking and cleaning; the child also remembers their mother offering emotional comfort while growing up. No treatment is named. ^q3

## What made it worse

The child describes guilt about moving and studying, helplessness as their mother's pain and tiredness worsen, and sadness when illness prevents a family celebration. They report that the mother has few visitors. ^q4
